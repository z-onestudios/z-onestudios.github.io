About Z-One

A home
for builders.

"Started as a group chat.
Now we ship real things." — the founders

Z-One began as a conversation between friends who believed great design and clean code could coexist — and that young people should be the ones building it.

We're lean, opinionated, and fast. No bloat, no hierarchy. Just talented individuals who care deeply about craft and shipping things that leave a mark.
