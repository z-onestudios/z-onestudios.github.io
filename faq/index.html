❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers.

Everything you need to know about working with Z-One — pricing, tech stack, 3D WebGL workflows, and project timelines.

01

How much does a custom website or WebGL project cost?

Project costs depend on scope, custom 3D requirements, backend architecture, and design complexity. We don't do hourly billing traps — we give you a transparent, fixed-scope proposal before writing a single line of code.

02

Are 3D WebGL websites mobile-friendly and fast?

Yes. We optimize all 3D canvas models using Draco compression, geometry instancing, dynamic pixel ratios, and touch controls to guarantee 60fps performance across modern mobile devices.

03

Which technologies do you use for web builds?

Our core stack centers around Next.js, React, Three.js, WebGL, Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Supabase, and TailwindCSS — chosen specifically for speed, security, and long-term scalability.

04

How fast can Z-One deliver a startup MVP?

Our dedicated MVP sprints take between 4 to 6 weeks from initial scope mapping to production deployment, completely ready for real users and investor pitches.

05

Who owns the codebase and design assets upon completion?

You do. You retain 100% full IP ownership of all design files, source code, database schemas, and server configurations upon final delivery.

06

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes, we offer retainer plans for ongoing feature expansions, server maintenance, performance audits, and continuous security updates.
