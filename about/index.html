Creative Studio & Engineering

Building Digital Experiences That Stand Out.

We build custom web products, 3D experiences, and modern software for visionary founders worldwide.

Z-One is a creative web development agency based in Srinagar, Kashmir, engineering custom websites, immersive 3D web experiences, startup platforms, and high-performance digital products for ambitious businesses across India and internationally. We reject slow templates, cookie-cutter frameworks, and generic marketing copy to focus entirely on precision web craft.

Our tech stack revolves around modern, resilient technologies including Next.js, React, Three.js, WebGL, Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and Supabase. By combining interactive real-time 3D graphics with rock-solid server-side architecture, we build interfaces that captivate users, load instantly, and scale smoothly as founders grow their companies.

Whether you are launching a seed-stage startup MVP, reinventing your visual brand identity, or deploying a high-throughput enterprise platform, Z-One delivers opinionated design systems and clean codebases built to stand out in crowded global markets.
